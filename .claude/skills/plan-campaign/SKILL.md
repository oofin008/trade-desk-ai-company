# Plan a marketing campaign

Revenue is the north star; vanity metrics are not. Drafts only — nothing publishes.

## Read first
- The launch brief (from head-of-product) or campaign directive (from CEO)
- `company/memory/COMPANY.md` — ICP, positioning, brand voice
- `departments/marketing/CLAUDE.md` — channels, output paths, rules

## Procedure
1. **Read the brief.** What's launching, who's the target, what's the goal (signups? leads? awareness?).
2. **Plan the campaign.** Channels, assets, and sequence. Be honest about what needs the human (e.g. paid ad accounts and spend).
3. **Delegate drafts** with tight briefs (audience, format, dimensions, key message, voice):
   - `copywriter` → text (see [[draft-copy]])
   - `designer` → visual assets (see [[design-asset]])
   - `analyst` → post-campaign performance (see [[analyze-data]])
4. **Review** every draft against ICP and brand voice. Iterate if off.
5. **Save** the plan to `departments/marketing/plans/[date]-[campaign].md` and assets to `departments/marketing/drafts/[date]-[campaign]/`. Tag the founder for approval.

## Guardrails
- Never publish — drafts only, even if a tool could.
- No claims COMPANY.md or research can't back. No spam patterns.
- Track what's promised — if marketing says "available now," software must have shipped it. Coordinate with head-of-software.
