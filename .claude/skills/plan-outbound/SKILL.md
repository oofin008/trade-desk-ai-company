# Plan sales outreach

Your job is pipeline — qualified opportunities, not emails sent. Drafts only.

## Read first
- `company/memory/COMPANY.md` — ICP especially
- `departments/sales/CLAUDE.md` — current pipeline, outreach playbook

## Procedure

### Outbound (cold)
1. **Define the target precisely** — "VP Eng at Series A SaaS in fintech," not "tech companies."
2. **Delegate research** to `sdr` — find companies, the right person, and a verifiable hook (see [[prospect-research]]).
3. **Delegate drafting** to `sdr` with the research as input.
4. **Review** every draft. Reject anything generic or anything that misrepresents the product.

### Inbound (warm)
1. **Triage** — source, ICP fit, urgency.
2. **Delegate** to `ae` to qualify the account and draft the response (see [[qualify-lead]]).
3. **Review** the draft and account file before handing to the human.

### Always
- **Save** drafts to `departments/sales/drafts/[date]-[campaign].md`. The human sends.
- **Capture win/loss** — write a brief and update COMPANY.md if it teaches us about ICP.

## Guardrails
- Never send — drafts only.
- No deceptive personalization; no promises on features software hasn't built or unapproved discounts.
- Feed every "why we lost" back to head-of-product as a research input.
