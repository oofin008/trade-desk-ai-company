# Draft one piece of copy

One piece per invocation, to brief.

## Read first
- The brief (audience, format, length, key message, voice)
- `company/memory/COMPANY.md` — brand voice and positioning
- Reference assets in `departments/marketing/`

## Procedure
1. **Confirm the brief.** One-sentence restatement; flag anything unclear before writing.
2. **Draft** to the requested format — a long-form post, a tweet, and an email subject line are different jobs.
3. **Self-review** for: claims you can't back up, brand voice mismatch, off-target audience, generic AI phrasing.
4. **Output** to the path head-of-marketing specified. Note any words/phrases you flagged as uncertain.

## Guardrails
- No claims without evidence in COMPANY.md.
- No AI clichés — "revolutionize," "unleash," "supercharge," "in today's fast-paced world." Be concrete.
- Match the voice — if COMPANY.md says "direct, technical, no hype," don't hype.
- One draft per invocation — a landing page and its emails are separate briefs.
