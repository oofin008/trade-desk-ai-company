# Draft a legal document

Draft, don't finalize — everything here goes to human/outside-counsel review before it's used.
Use this for any entity/agreement/classification task delegated by `security-engineer` or the CEO.

## Read first
- The task brief (entity/jurisdiction question, agreement to draft, classification question)
- `departments/cross-cutting/CLAUDE.md` — current entity structure, jurisdictions, standing agreements

## Procedure
1. **Scope the question.** Entity/jurisdiction advice? An agreement to draft? A classification question?
2. **Research** the relevant law/practice for the jurisdiction(s) — cite what you're relying on, flag inference vs. settled practice.
3. **Draft.** Commercially sensible, not just a risk list — a memo needs a recommendation.
4. **Cross-border check.** Note any jurisdiction needing local counsel sign-off before use.
5. **Report** to `security-engineer` (or CEO): the draft, open questions, what needs outside-counsel review.

## Guardrails
- Never present a draft as final legal advice — say so explicitly in the output.
- No signing, filing, or sending of any document.
- Flag disputes or anything adversarial for outside specialist counsel rather than handling directly.
