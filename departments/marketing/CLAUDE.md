# Marketing Department

## What this department owns
- Launches: announcing what Product/Software ships
- Content: blog, landing pages, social
- Demand gen: campaigns that bring qualified leads to Sales

## What it does NOT own
- Brand strategy (lives in COMPANY.md, CEO/founder decides)
- Product claims (Product team owns what's true)
- Ad spend (founder decides budgets)

## Outputs
- **Campaign plans** → `departments/marketing/plans/[date]-[campaign].md`
- **Draft assets** → `departments/marketing/drafts/[date]-[campaign]/`
- **Performance recaps** → `departments/marketing/recaps/[date]-[campaign].md`

## Channels
> ⚠️ Set the real channels for your ICP via **`/scaffold-company`** or by editing here.
Pick the channels where your ICP actually makes decisions — don't spread thin.
- **<Primary channel>** — <why it fits the ICP>
- **<Secondary channel>** — <why>
- **<Blog / landing page>** — SEO and demo content
- **<Email>** — for prospects who opt in
- Deprioritize channels your ICP doesn't use; don't plan campaigns there unless the founder directs.

## Rules
- Brand voice rules in COMPANY.md are non-negotiable.
- Every claim is back-able with evidence from COMPANY.md or research.
- No publishing without founder approval.
