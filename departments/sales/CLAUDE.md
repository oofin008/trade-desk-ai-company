# Sales Department

## What this department owns
- Prospecting: finding and qualifying potential customers
- Outreach: drafting personalized outreach to targets
- Pipeline tracking: who's in motion, who closed/lost, why
- Feedback to Product: what prospects say about the product

## What it does NOT own
- Pricing (CEO/founder)
- Discounts (CEO/founder)
- Actually sending messages (human does)

## Outputs
- **Account research** → `departments/sales/research/[date]-[campaign].md`
- **Outreach drafts** → `departments/sales/drafts/[date]-[campaign].md`
- **Pipeline updates** → `departments/sales/PIPELINE.md` (kept current)
- **Win/loss notes** → `departments/sales/win-loss/[date]-[account].md`

## Outreach playbook
- Cold email: ≤100 words. Personal hook (verifiable). Clear ask. No fluff.
- LinkedIn: shorter than email. Connection request + brief message.
- Follow-up: max 2 follow-ups before disengaging. Vary the angle.

## Rules
- No fake personalization.
- No promises beyond what Product confirmed.
- Every "no" gets a brief reason logged for Product to learn from.
