Front Office

Owns risk, execution & P&L
FO-01Core

Head of Trading / Desk Lead

Owns the trading strategy, the risk mandate, and the desk's bottom line.

Responsibilities

  • Set the desk's mandate: which markets, strategies, and instruments are in scope and which are off-limits.
  • Own aggregate P&L and defend it to investors, partners, or the board.
  • Allocate risk budget and capital across traders and strategies.
  • Approve new strategies, venues, and counterparties before they go live.
  • Set escalation rules — when a trader must cut, hedge, or call for sign-off.

Must have

  • Live P&L track record on a crypto or derivatives desk.
  • Fluency in perps, funding, basis, and liquidation mechanics.
  • Judgment under drawdown — the temperament to size down, not double up.
Reports to
Founder / CIO
Hire when
Day 1
Breaks desk if missing
Total — no mandate owner
FO-02Core

Trader

Executes the strategy — discretionary, systematic, or market-making.

Responsibilities

  • Work orders across spot, perps, and options with an eye on slippage and market impact.
  • Run the assigned book: directional, arbitrage, market-making, or basis, within set limits.
  • Monitor funding, open interest, and liquidity in real time; adjust exposure accordingly.
  • Manage inventory and hedges; flatten or reduce ahead of known events.
  • Log every fill and rationale for post-trade review and attribution.

Must have

  • Screen time on crypto venues and comfort with high volatility.
  • Discipline to trade the plan and respect stops and limits.
  • Numeracy for on-the-fly sizing, funding math, and risk-reward.
Reports to
Head of Trading
Hire when
Day 1 (may be the lead early)
Breaks desk if missing
No execution
FO-03Early

Quant Researcher / Quant Developer

Turns market data into signals, models, and tested strategies.

Responsibilities

  • Research alpha: build, backtest, and validate signals across regimes.
  • Stand up the data pipeline — historical and live market, funding, and on-chain feeds.
  • Build the backtesting and simulation stack with realistic fees, slippage, and latency.
  • Translate research into production strategy code and monitor live-vs-backtest drift.
  • Size positions and set risk parameters from statistical evidence, not gut.

Must have

  • Strong Python (or equivalent) plus statistics and time-series skill.
  • Backtesting rigor — awareness of overfitting, look-ahead, and survivorship bias.
  • Ability to ship research into running, monitored code.
Reports to
Head of Trading
Hire when
Once strategy needs to scale
Breaks desk if missing
No edge beyond discretion
FO-04Early

Execution / Trading Infrastructure Engineer

Keeps the pipes fast, connected, and always up.

Responsibilities

  • Build and maintain exchange connectivity — REST and WebSocket, order routing, market data.
  • Own uptime, latency, and failover for the trading stack.
  • Build execution algos and the order-management layer traders rely on.
  • Instrument monitoring, alerting, and kill-switches for runaway or stuck orders.
  • Manage deployment, secrets, and environment separation between test and live.

Must have

  • Production experience with low-latency, event-driven systems.
  • Fluency with exchange APIs, rate limits, and reconnection edge cases.
  • DevOps discipline — observability, on-call, and incident response.
Reports to
Head of Trading / CTO
Hire when
Before automating flow
Breaks desk if missing
Downtime = missed / stuck orders
MO

Middle Office

Independent check on risk & conduct
MO-01Core

Risk Manager

Independent guardrail on exposure, leverage, and liquidation risk.

Responsibilities

  • Set and enforce position, leverage, and concentration limits per trader and book.
  • Monitor margin, liquidation distance, and exposure across venues in real time.
  • Run stress tests and scenario analysis — gap moves, venue outages, funding shocks.
  • Own the risk dashboard and escalate breaches independently of the desk.
  • Model counterparty and venue risk; cap exposure to any single exchange.

Must have

  • Quantitative risk background — VaR, stress testing, margin systems.
  • Deep grasp of derivatives leverage and forced-liquidation mechanics.
  • Independence — reports outside the trading P&L line.
Reports to
Founder / board — NOT the desk
Hire when
Before real leverage is live
Breaks desk if missing
Blow-up risk — the classic killer
MO-02Early

Compliance Officer

Keeps the desk inside the law across every jurisdiction it touches.

Responsibilities

  • Own KYC/AML onboarding for counterparties, venues, and (if applicable) clients.
  • Track licensing and registration requirements per market and file what's required.
  • Screen against sanctions and monitor transactions for suspicious activity.
  • Write and enforce internal policies — personal trading, conflicts, market conduct.
  • Manage regulator relationships, audits, and reporting obligations.

Must have

  • Crypto/fintech regulatory knowledge in the desk's jurisdictions.
  • AML/KYC program experience and sanctions-screening fluency.
  • Willingness to say no and halt activity when rules require it.
Reports to
Founder / board
Hire when
Before trading regulated markets — often fractional first
Breaks desk if missing
Legal / licensing exposure
BO

Back Office

Settlement, treasury & the books
BO-01Early

Operations / Settlement

Reconciles every trade, transfer, and balance so the numbers are true.

Responsibilities

  • Reconcile trades, fills, and balances across all venues daily.
  • Manage deposits, withdrawals, and wallet/custody operations under strict controls.
  • Run fiat on/off ramps and banking relationships.
  • Investigate and resolve breaks between internal records and exchange statements.
  • Maintain segregation of duties — ops moves funds, trading never does.

Must have

  • Reconciliation discipline and comfort with crypto transfer mechanics.
  • Process rigor — checklists, dual approval, audit trails.
  • Zero tolerance for unexplained breaks.
Reports to
COO / Head of Ops
Hire when
As soon as capital moves regularly
Breaks desk if missing
Untracked funds / broken books
BO-02Scale

Treasury

Manages where capital sits and makes idle balances work.

Responsibilities

  • Allocate capital across venues to meet margin needs without over-funding any one.
  • Manage funding, rehypothecation, and yield on idle balances within risk limits.
  • Forecast liquidity and cash-flow needs across the desk.
  • Optimize stablecoin and fiat holdings; manage FX where relevant.
  • Coordinate with risk on venue-exposure caps and collateral placement.

Must have

  • Treasury or liquidity-management background.
  • Understanding of exchange collateral, margin, and settlement cycles.
  • Caution around counterparty risk on parked balances.
Reports to
CFO / COO
Hire when
When capital spans many venues
Breaks desk if missing
Trapped capital, funding drag
BO-03Scale

Accounting / Finance

Owns the books, tax, and audit-ready records.

Responsibilities

  • Maintain the general ledger and produce P&L and balance-sheet statements.
  • Handle crypto tax treatment, cost-basis tracking, and filings.
  • Prepare for and support external audits.
  • Track fees, funding, and financing costs against strategy performance.
  • Build management reporting for partners and investors.

Must have

  • Accounting qualification and crypto-accounting familiarity.
  • Grasp of digital-asset cost-basis and tax complexity.
  • Precision and documentation habits.
Reports to
CFO
Hire when
Often outsourced first, in-house at scale
Breaks desk if missing
Tax / audit exposure

SECCore

Security Engineer

Guards the keys — the one failure that ends the firm.

Responsibilities

  • Design custody architecture: cold/hot wallet split, multisig, HSMs, key ceremonies.
  • Own key management, rotation, and recovery procedures.
  • Enforce withdrawal controls — allowlists, thresholds, multi-party approval.
  • Run access control, secrets management, and infrastructure hardening.
  • Lead incident response for compromise, phishing, and insider-threat scenarios.

Must have

  • Applied crypto-security and custody experience.
  • Adversarial mindset — assumes attackers, insiders, and mistakes.
  • Discipline to make security non-optional even under time pressure.
Reports to
CTO / Founder
Hire when
Day 1 — never skimp, even lean
Breaks desk if missing
One breach = game over
LEGScale

Legal Counsel

Structures the entity and every agreement the desk signs.

Responsibilities

  • Advise on entity structure, jurisdiction, and licensing strategy.
  • Draft and negotiate counterparty, prime-broker, and venue agreements.
  • Advise on regulatory classification of instruments and activities.
  • Handle IP, employment, and investor documentation.
  • Manage disputes and liaise with external specialist counsel.

Must have

  • Financial-services / digital-asset legal background.
  • Cross-border structuring experience.
  • Commercial judgment, not just risk-flagging.
Reports to
Founder / board
Hire when
External counsel early, in-house at scale
Breaks desk if missing
Structural / contractual exposure
Minimum viable desk

The lean version — 3 to 5 people

You do not staff all eleven seats on day one. Most functions collapse into a handful of people wearing several hats — but two things you cannot compress: security/custody and independent risk. Those are where crypto desks blow up.
